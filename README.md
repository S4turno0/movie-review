## 🎉 Features

The movie-review application offers various features to enhance your experience:

- **User-Friendly Interface**: Easily navigate through your reviews and ratings.
- **Search Functionality**: Quickly find movies by title, genre, or rating.
- **Review History**: Keep track of all your previous reviews.

## 🌟 How to Use

After installing, open the movie-review application. Here’s how to use it:

1. **Create an Account**: Sign up to save your reviews.
2. **Add a Movie**: Click on "Add Movie" and fill in the details like title, genre, and your rating.
3. **Write a Review**: Share your thoughts in the review section.
4. **Submit Your Review**: Click "Submit" to save your movie review.
